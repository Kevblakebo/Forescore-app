Common Questions

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can start or join a round instantly with just a round or tournament code - no account, no download required for anyone else in your group. Creating an account is optional, and unlocks things like GPS distance, saved stats, and group leaderboards.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at support@ripscoregolf.com from the address on your account and we'll remove your account and associated personal data.

A round or score isn't syncing correctly. What do I do?

First, check that everyone in the round has a working internet connection - RipScore syncs scores live between everyone playing. If a round still looks wrong after that, email us with the round code and a quick description of what you're seeing, and we'll take a look.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

We'd genuinely love to hear it - email us at support@ripscoregolf.com with as much detail as you can (what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead). Screenshots help a lot if you have one.

Delete Your Account

You can request permanent deletion of your RipScore account and all associated personal data at any time. This page works whether or not you currently have the app installed.

How to request deletion

Send an email to the address below from the email you used to sign up for RipScore, with the subject line "Delete My Account":

Email support@ripscoregolf.com
We ask you to email from your account's own address so we can confirm the request is genuinely coming from the account holder, rather than deleting an account at someone else's request.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, email, and login credentials
  • Your profile (name, handicap, home course, Venmo handle, avatar)
  • Your personal round history and stats
  • Your membership in any groups

How long it takes

We process deletion requests within 7 days of receiving your email. You'll get a confirmation once it's complete.

What we may retain

Scores you've entered in a shared round with other players may remain visible to those other players as part of that round's history, since the round itself belongs to the group that played it, not to any one player. Your own personal identity is removed from our systems; the round data itself isn't retroactively deleted from other players' history just because one participant deleted their account.

Questions about this process? Contact support@ripscoregolf.com.
